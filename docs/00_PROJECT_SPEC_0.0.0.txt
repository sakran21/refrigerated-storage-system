DOCUMENT: Project Specification
VERSION: 0.0.0
STATUS: Pre-Design

==================================================
1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
==================================================

This project is a web-based management and oversight system for a refrigerated storage unit rental business.

The system replaces paper-based tracking and provides a centralized source of truth for customers, storage units, rentals, billing periods, electricity usage, charges, payments, balances, delinquency, manual overrides, and operational oversight.

The application is intended for real daily operational use. It must remain simple enough for non-technical staff while providing sufficient visibility, traceability, and auditability for ownership and oversight.

The system is not being built as a lightweight demo. It is being designed as a maintainable business application with production-oriented structure, data persistence, and clear operational rules.

==================================================
2. PRIMARY BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
==================================================

The primary objective of the system is operational control and visibility.

The owner or stakeholder should be able to determine:

- Which units are occupied
- Which units are available
- Which units are in maintenance
- Which customers are associated with each unit
- What money is owed
- What money has been received
- Which rentals are delinquent
- What electricity charges have been incurred
- What payments have been recorded
- What overrides or unusual actions have occurred
- What gross cash was received during a calendar month

The system should reduce dependence on paper records and minimize opportunities for records to be altered without accountability.

==================================================
6. BUSINESS DOMAIN
==================================================

The business rents refrigerated storage units.

Customers rent units on recurring monthly billing periods.

Customers may:
- Rent multiple units
- Pay late
- Make partial payments
- Have multiple active rentals

Each rental is unit-centric.

One rental represents one unit rental period from start to closure.

Electricity consumption is tracked separately and billed according to recorded meter readings.

The business primarily operates with cash payments.

Late payments are expected in this business and should not automatically be treated as suspicious behavior.

==================================================
8. CORE BUSINESS PRINCIPLES
==================================================

Charges and payments are separate concepts.

A charge represents money owed.

A payment represents cash received.

Late payments are expected and should not automatically be considered suspicious.

Delinquency is a business state, not evidence of misconduct.

Manual overrides are allowed when operationally necessary, but they must be logged and reviewable.

Historical records should remain traceable.

Financial and operational records should not be silently deleted or rewritten.

Notes may provide human context, but notes must not act as the only mechanism for changing money, status, billing, delinquency, or reporting logic.

Operational transparency is more important than blind automation.

The system should favor simplicity whenever a more complex implementation provides little practical business value.
