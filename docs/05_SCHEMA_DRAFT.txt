2. SCHEMA PRINCIPLES
==================================================

SP-001
The database must preserve historical business records.

SP-002
Financial records must not be silently deleted or overwritten by Operator.

SP-003
Charges and payments are separate concepts.

SP-004
Payments reduce rental-level balance.

SP-005
Payment allocations are removed from v1.

SP-006
Electricity rate is stored as a system setting in v1.

SP-007
Charges store electricity_rate_snapshot for historical accuracy.

SP-008
Rental_Customers is removed from v1.

SP-009
One rental has one responsible customer.

SP-010
Audit logs are action history only.

SP-011
Review flags are high-signal Admin review markers.

SP-012
Primary control comes from locked records, append-only notes, review flags, configurable sensitive-action settings, and Admin-only correction workflow.

SP-013
Notes are stored in one generic notes table.

SP-014
Notes are contextual only and must not be used as the only mechanism for changing business state.

SP-015
Admin corrections are tracked in a dedicated admin_corrections table.

SP-016
Operator cannot override generated charges.

SP-017
Operator can trigger charge generation for eligible rentals and billing periods.

SP-018
Generated charge values are calculated by the system and locked after creation.

SP-019
Charge overrides, charge voids, payment corrections, meter reading corrections, rental reopening, and early rental closure overrides are Admin correction workflows.

18. DERIVED VALUES AND CALCULATIONS
==================================================

Outstanding Balance:
- Calculated at rental level.

Formula:
total charges - total payments - applied deposit = outstanding balance

Total Charges:
- Sum of non-voided charges for rental.

Total Payments:
- Sum of locked payments for rental.

Applied Deposit:
- Sum of applied deposit amount for rental.

Revenue:
- Calendar-month cash received.
- Deposits are not revenue.

Rent Charges:
- Sum of rent charges.

Electricity Charges:
- Sum of electricity charges.

Delinquency:
- Rental is delinquent when unpaid balance exists after due date + grace period.

Meter Reading Completion:
- Derived by checking whether a meter reading exists for the billing period.

Backdated Payment:
- payment_date is earlier than entered_at date.
